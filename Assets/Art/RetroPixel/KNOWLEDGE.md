# RetroPixel/

Original pixel-art sprites used to give the gameplay a cohesive retro look.

## Files
### `RetroGround.asset`
Repeating dark pixel-art ground tile used by the gameplay scene.

### `RetroPlayer.asset`
Pixel-art player character sprite used by the gameplay scene.

### `RetroZombie.asset`
Default pixel-art sprite for standard zombies.

### `RetroRunner.asset`
Pixel-art sprite for fast zombie variants.

### `RetroBrute.asset`
Pixel-art sprite for tough zombie variants.

### `RetroBoss.asset`
Pixel-art sprite for fifth-wave boss zombies.

### `RetroBolt.asset`
Pixel-art projectile sprite used by the bullet prefab.
