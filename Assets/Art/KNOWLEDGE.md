# Art/

Visual assets used by the game, grouped by their source and style.

## Subfolders
- `Kenney/` — CC0 top-down shooter sprite pack. See [`Kenney/KNOWLEDGE.md`](Kenney/KNOWLEDGE.md).
- `RetroPixel/` — original procedural pixel-art character, enemy, projectile, and ground sprites. See [`RetroPixel/KNOWLEDGE.md`](RetroPixel/KNOWLEDGE.md).
